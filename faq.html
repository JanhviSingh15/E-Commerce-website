How Can We Help You?

Find quick answers to your questions about our luxury collections, express shipping, returns, and bespoke concierge services.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery

We offer complimentary standard express shipping on all domestic orders over $100. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days. Expedited overnight luxury delivery is available at checkout for a flat rate of $25. All orders are hand-packaged in our signature LUXE luxury boxes with climate protection.
Yes, LUXE ships globally to over 120 countries via DHL Express and FedEx International Priority. International shipping costs and duties/taxes are calculated transparently at checkout with no hidden arrival fees.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 30-day complimentary return policy for all unused, unworn items in their original condition with authentic tags intact. Simply initiate a return through your account or contact our concierge team to receive a prepaid courier shipping label.
Once received and verified at our quality control center, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days back to your original payment method. You will receive an instant email notification confirming your refund confirmation.

📐 Sizing & Product Authenticity

100% Guaranteed. Every piece in our boutique is sourced directly from master ateliers in Milan, Paris, Florence, and Tokyo. All items undergo rigorous multi-step serial number and material verification by expert luxury authenticators before shipment.
Each product page includes an extensive size guide with exact garment measurements in inches and centimeters. If you are between sizes, our fit recommendation advisor recommends sizing up for structured coats or contacting our fit specialists for personalized advice.

Still Have Questions?

Our dedicated Client Concierge team is available 24/7 to assist with sizing advice, bespoke orders, and order inquiries.
